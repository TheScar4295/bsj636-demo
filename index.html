Welcome to Lodge Buchan St. John No. 636

The purpose of this web site is to pass on information about Lodge Buchan St John No. 636.

The Lodge meets in Broxburn, West Lothian, Scotland on the first and third Thursday of every month between September and April.

This Web Site like Freemasonry is dynamic, and will always have new information added, so please bookmark this page and come back again.

Thanks for visiting our site,Brother Alan DillettMaster 2025-26

A welcome from the new Master, Brother Derek Meek, will follow.
